QUESTIONS

Useful things to know.

Can Docora edit text that is already inside a PDF?+

Docora can detect many text items and replace them visually. Scans, outlined lettering, unusual embedded fonts and complex layouts may require whiteout-and-replace or a future OCR workflow.

Does Docora upload my PDF?+

The document is handled locally in the browser. Third-party libraries are currently delivered through established content delivery networks, but the PDF file itself is not sent to them by Docora.

Can I continue editing later?+

Yes. Docora autosaves the current session locally and can export an editable .docora project containing the PDF and editing data.

Does it work on phones and tablets?+

The interface adapts to smaller screens with slide-out page and properties panels, a bottom tool strip and touch zoom support. Desktop remains the best option for precise document work.
